About the Project

Transparent and anonymous voting system

How it works

This system uses Blind Signatures to ensure that your identity is completely disconnected from your vote. The voting server signs your ballot without seeing its content.

  • Anonymity: Not even the administrator can see who you voted for.
  • Integrity: Each vote is cryptographically signed and cannot be altered.
  • Transparency: Once closed, results are public and verifiable.
